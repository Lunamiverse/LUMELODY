All sounds are original synthesis from the LUMELODY engine (the plugin's C++ code, compiled for the browser); every key plays the selected sound at its own pitch. All 20 effects run in that engine and pass 184 automated checks. The moon, knobs and foil are rendered live in WebGL and react to the notes you play and to your pointer; without WebGL they fall back to still drawings. Recorded acoustic instruments are not included yet.
