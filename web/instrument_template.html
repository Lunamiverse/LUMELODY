Off

All sounds are original synthesis from the LUMELODY engine (the plugin's C++ code, compiled for the browser); every key is synthesized at its own pitch. All 20 effects run in that engine and pass 184 automated checks. The holographic foil, chrome knobs and logo are rendered live in WebGL and react to your pointer; they approximate real foil and fall back to a still gradient without WebGL. Recorded acoustic instruments are not included yet.
