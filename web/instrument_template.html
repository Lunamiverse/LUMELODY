Sound library

Every sound is original synthesis from the LUMELODY engine, the same C++ code that runs in the plugin, compiled for your browser. Each key is synthesized at its own pitch, so nothing is a stretched sample. All 20 effects run in that engine and were tested automatically (184 checks). Real acoustic piano, guitar, brass, orchestral strings and Brazilian percussion still need licensed or recorded sources; the guitar, string, choir and horn sounds here are synthesized and say so. The knobs are rendered live on your GPU with a thin-film shader; if WebGL is unavailable they fall back to a flat drawing.
