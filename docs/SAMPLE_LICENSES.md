## What the Dembow Dominicano and Reggaetón sounds are, and are not

The tambora (palito, mano, tapao, cáscara, redoble) and güira (raspado, corto) sounds are **physical models**: synthesized
imitations of how those instruments vibrate (a two-headed drum with a wooden shell; a scraped metal cylinder). They are
not recordings of Dominican tamboras or güiras, and the plugin describes them as "physical model" so nobody mistakes them
for recorded instruments. The patterns are merengue- and dembow-rooted figures written for this library; they do not copy
any song.

If recorded instruments are added later, each file gets a row above with its recording source, the player's licence and
proof of the right to redistribute it inside the plugin.
